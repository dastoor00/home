01 — The heart of Dastoor

Old roots.
New hands.

Dastoor takes its name from the customs and traditions that make a place feel like home. We believe heritage isn’t something to simply look back at — it’s something we can keep creating, together.

دستورTradition lives on

Dastoor is a student-led business born from a shared love for the beauty of Kashmiri culture. Inspired by its rich patterns, thoughtful craftsmanship, and timeless stories, we’re learning how to honour tradition while adding a touch of our own imagination.

Every idea begins with curiosity. Every detail is an opportunity to learn. And every step — from exploring materials to shaping a design — reminds us that meaningful things are made with patience, care, and a little courage.

“Heritage is not just what we inherit. It is what we choose to keep alive.”
